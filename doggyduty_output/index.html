Questions

Answers before you take this to the board

Which areas do you service?

All of Central Florida, including Tampa, Orlando, Jacksonville, Lakeland, Kissimmee, Winter Haven and Ocala. If you are just outside those lines, call us and we will tell you honestly whether a route reaches you.

How does valet trash pickup work?

Residents leave a tied bag in the provided container outside their door during the evening window. A uniformed technician collects it and walks it to your compactor or dumpster. Nothing sits out overnight.

How often are pet waste stations serviced?

Once per week or twice per week suits most communities. Larger properties and dog parks often run three visits or a nightly rotation during season.

Do you supply the waste bags?

Yes. Every station is restocked with fresh rolls on every visit at no extra charge, and we clear and wipe down the station area while we are there.

Do you install the stations?

Yes. We install the Doggy Duty pedal dispenser with glow signage, position it where residents actually walk, and the station body carries a lifetime warranty.

What happens if a visit is missed?

We return within 24 hours and credit the visit. If we miss more than two scheduled visits in a month, that month is free. That promise is written into the agreement.

How long is the agreement?

Standard agreements run twelve months and can be cancelled with 30 days written notice. Seasonal terms and short trials are available for boards that want proof first.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Doggy Duty, LLC is licensed and insured, and we send a certificate of insurance for your board packet before the first visit.

Who do residents contact about service?

Residents can email or call us directly, which keeps the office phone quiet. Your manager still receives the written service log after every route.
