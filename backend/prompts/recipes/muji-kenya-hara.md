# muji-kenya-hara — Editorial / Minimalist (quiet)

**Vibe**: Emptiness as fullness — warm paper off-white, near-silence, no ornament.
**Best for**: houseware/object retail, slow-living or craft-goods storefronts, any brand whose tone is "calm", "natural", "quiet", "minimal", "handcrafted" without needing to shout.

## Palette approach
- Ground: warm paper off-white (never pure white).
- Ink: warm near-black (never pure black).
- No decorative accent color by default — if one is truly needed, a single brand color used only as a tiny corner mark or rule.

## Typography
- Display and body in a single humanist sans, weight 400, never bolder than 500, slightly open letter-spacing -- Manrope fits well.
- Body at 15-16px, generous line-height (~1.8).

## Spacing
8 / 16 / 32 / 48 / 96 / 160 — generous section gaps matter; don't be afraid of very large vertical gaps.

## Radius
0. Hard, honest edges (maybe 2px on form fields).

## Shadow
None.

## Motion
Imperceptible — fades over 600-900ms, nothing translates more than a few pixels, never bouncy.

## Signature moves
- The majority of the page is empty space; content lives in a narrow column, away from edges.
- One image per section, modestly sized, plainly captioned.
- Tiny letter-spaced labels naming each section ("01 — Our Story") instead of shouting headlines.
- 1px hairline rules are the only ornament.

## Avoid
- Any saturated color beyond a single restrained accent mark.
- Drop shadows, glows, gradients of any kind.
- Tabs, accordions, mega-menus, sticky banners.
- Assuming everything important must be "above the fold".
