# aesop — Editorial / Minimalist (apothecary refinement)

**Vibe**: Apothecary refinement — warm chamois ground, sage, ink, serif copy that reads like a literary magazine.
**Best for**: premium retail/boutique, skincare/wellness/beauty, restaurants and hospitality with a refined voice — tone words like "warm", "premium", "quiet", "refined", "artisan".

## Palette approach
- Ground: warm chamois/off-white (never pure white).
- Ink: true near-black for copy (not pure black).
- One muted sage/green as a secondary, one deep amber/brown as a sparingly-used accent (a single rule or seal mark, not a flood fill).

## Typography
- Display and body in a single transitional serif at every size (regular weight, italic available) -- Newsreader fits well -- copy reads like product writing in a magazine, long well-cadenced sentences.
- A single sans paired in for UI labels/navigation only -- Work Sans.
- Small-caps section labels, letter-spaced.

## Spacing
8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 72 / 120.

## Radius
0 throughout, including form fields — square-edged.

## Shadow
None.

## Motion
Subtle fades and slow drawer/slide transitions (~450ms ease-out). Never spring-bounce.

## Signature moves
- Long-form, well-written body copy instead of punchy marketing fragments.
- Product/subject photographed simply with generous negative space and a long soft shadow.
- Small-caps section labels above each block.
- Asymmetric layouts — image on one side, generous margin of copy on the other; avoid centered-everything.

## Avoid
- Bold font weights anywhere — stays regular and italic.
- Any color outside sage / amber / ink — no blues, no purples, no UI-blue accents.
- Pop-up modals.
- Stock photography of smiling models.
