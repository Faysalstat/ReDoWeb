# mailchimp-freddie — Warm Humanist (friendly SMB)

**Vibe**: Hand-drawn warmth, a flood of one warm brand color, personality in the copy.
**Best for**: local/small businesses, creator or community products, approachable consumer services — tone words like "friendly", "warm", "approachable", "fun", "personal".

## Palette approach
- One warm, saturated brand color used as a flood-fill section background (hero/hero-adjacent), white for body sections.
- Warm near-black ink (never pure black).
- A warm cream surface for secondary sections.
- One accent (a warm pink/coral) used very sparingly.

## Typography
- Display: a friendly grotesque, weight 700-800 -- Plus Jakarta Sans fits well.
- Body: same family, 17-18px, weight 400, line-height 1.6.

## Spacing
4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 — generous but not overly airy.

## Radius
8 (small) / 16 (medium) / 24 (large/pill). Buttons are pill-shaped.

## Shadow
Soft and warm, e.g. `0 4px 12px rgba(36, 28, 21, 0.08)`.

## Motion
Bouncy is welcome here — spring-style hover lifts, friendly easing.

## Signature moves
- Flood-fill brand-color section backgrounds that break up the page rhythm.
- Pill-shaped buttons, optionally with a bold outline.
- Personality in the microcopy ("Come say hi" rather than "Contact us").
- Slightly asymmetric/"tilted" placement of supporting visuals rather than perfectly centered grids.

## Avoid
- Pure black anywhere — stay warm near-black.
- More than 3 colors visible at once (brand color + ink + one accent).
- Cold, corporate-sounding copy.
- Perfectly symmetric, centered-everything layouts.
