# vibrant-friendly — Custom (playful local business)

**Vibe**: Personal, adaptive, and colorful — tonal surfaces and soft curves rather than stark white-and-shadow cards.
**Best for**: local retail, food and hospitality, family-facing services — tone words like "playful", "colorful", "friendly", "vibrant", "fun", "approachable" where `mailchimp-freddie`'s single-flood-color approach feels too narrow.

## Palette approach
- The real brand primary color seeds a small family of tonal surfaces (light tint, mid, and a deep variant) used across backgrounds, not just accents — colorful without ever being a flat single-hue flood.
- Secondary/accent brand colors appear as chips, icon containers, and small highlight elements.
- Avoid stark pure-white backgrounds throughout — prefer a soft tonal neutral.

## Typography
- Display: a rounded, friendly sans, weight 600-700 -- Baloo 2 fits well.
- Body: Nunito, 16px, weight 400, comfortable line-height.

## Spacing
Comfortable, multiples of 4 — not as airy/generous as an editorial recipe, not as tight as `industrial-craft`.

## Radius
Generously rounded (16-24px) on cards and inputs; pill-shaped buttons are appropriate here.

## Shadow
Soft, tonal (tinted toward the surface's own hue) rather than neutral gray — colored elevation.

## Motion
Smooth and confident, never jarring — scale/lift hover states, a satisfying press feedback (`active:scale-95` equivalent) on buttons.

## Signature moves
- Tonal surface layering (light/mid/deep variants of the brand color) instead of stark white cards.
- Icon containers as solid tonal circles/rounded squares.
- Featured elements (e.g. a highlighted pricing tier or product) lifted with a small vertical offset for hierarchy.
- Tactile press feedback on every interactive element.

## Avoid
- Stark, shadow-less flat design (that's a different recipe's territory).
- A single hue used identically everywhere — vary tonal depth for hierarchy instead.
- Overly corporate, muted neutrals — this recipe should read as warm and colorful.
