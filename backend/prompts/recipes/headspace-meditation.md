# headspace-meditation — Warm Humanist (cozy)

**Vibe**: Soft pastels, rounded everything, a calming rather than energetic warmth.
**Best for**: wellness/health/coaching services, "approachable expert" positioning — tone words like "calm", "caring", "gentle", "cozy", "welcoming".

## Palette approach
- Ground: a warm peach/pastel tone.
- A darker warm surface color for contrast blocks.
- Warm dark teal (not cool navy) for ink.
- One or two muted pop accents (lavender, sage, salmon), one per section, rotating rather than combined.

## Typography
- Display: a rounded, friendly sans, weight 600 -- Quicksand fits well.
- Body: Nunito, 16-17px, weight 400.
- An occasional soft handwritten accent for a single emotional line, used sparingly -- Caveat.

## Spacing
8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96 — generous.

## Radius
Large: 16 (small) / 24 (medium) / 32 (large). Buttons are full pills.

## Shadow
Soft and warm, often tinted to match the ground, e.g. `0 8px 24px rgba(244, 165, 115, 0.2)`.

## Motion
Gentle and slow — 400-800ms eases, a "buoyant" hover lift rather than a snap. A subtle slow breathing/pulse animation on one key element is fine.

## Signature moves
- Rounded everything — every container is a pill or soft-corner rectangle.
- Warm gradient grounds that shift gently between hues.
- Calming, reassuring microcopy.
- One accent color per section rather than mixing several at once.

## Avoid
- Cool palettes — this recipe stays warm even in a dark variant.
- Sharp corners anywhere.
- Harsh or bouncy-spring motion — gentle eases only.
- More than 2 colors active in one section.
