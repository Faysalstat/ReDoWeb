# pentagram — Information Architecture (bold type)

**Vibe**: Bold typography as image; type carries the page, everything else recedes.
**Best for**: agencies/studios, professional/creative services, "we mean business" positioning — tone words like "bold", "confident", "graphic", "direct".

## Palette approach
- High-contrast two-color system: ink on ground (e.g. near-black on near-white, or one saturated brand color on a warm neutral).
- A single secondary accent only if truly needed, used very sparingly (e.g. one flat color-block section divider).

## Typography
- Display: a single bold grotesque sans used at massive scale for headlines -- Bricolage Grotesque fits well -- often set bigger than its column, intentionally breaking the margin.
- Body: the same family at a lighter weight, 16-17px, line-height 1.45-1.55.
- Tabular numerals from the same family for any stats.

## Spacing
Strict column grid with generous gutters (24-32px); baseline-aligned text blocks.

## Radius
0.

## Shadow
None.

## Motion
Typographic moves only — letters/headlines slide or shift in on a baseline. No 3D, no bounce, no parallax.

## Signature moves
- Headlines large enough to touch, or visibly break past, the column edge.
- A single word or short phrase at hero scale used as the primary graphic.
- Flat color-block section dividers, sometimes carrying a single large section number.
- Tiny precise captions/metadata pinned to the grid gutter.

## Avoid
- Photography leading the composition — if a hero photo exists, it sits behind/under large type.
- More than one type family.
- Gradients, shadows, or other decoration.
- Dense card grids — think in full-width spreads, not cards.
