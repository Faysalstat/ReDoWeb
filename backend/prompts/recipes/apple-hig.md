# apple-hig — Editorial / Minimalist (premium stage)

**Vibe**: Generous space, hero content on white, one thought per section.
**Best for**: premium consumer product/service businesses that want the content itself to feel like it deserves a stage — tone words like "premium", "polished", "confident", "serious".

## Palette approach
- Ground: paper-pure white (deep black reserved only for a hero/film moment, if any).
- Ink: near-black, not pure black.
- Soft neutral surface for secondary sections.
- One accent color used sparingly (a single link/CTA color, never flooding backgrounds).

## Typography
- Display: a refined system sans at weight 600 for headlines, 400 for sub-display -- Inter Tight fits well.
- Body: Inter at ~17px, line-height ~1.47.
- Captions in a muted gray, smaller size.

## Spacing
4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96 / 160 — section breaks are large; headlines get room to breathe.

## Radius
12 (small components) / 18 (cards) / 22 (large panels) — rarely sharp-cornered, never gummy.

## Shadow
Barely visible, `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)` at most — elevation comes from whitespace and contrast, not shadow.

## Motion
Expo-out easing; 350-650ms for layout moves, 150-250ms for hover. Never bouncy.

## Signature moves
- One strong image/visual centered on generous whitespace per section.
- Section anatomy: small eyebrow label -> big headline -> one paragraph -> one visual -> repeat. No tabs, no card grids, no bento layouts.
- Hairline dividers between sections, never anything heavier.
- A single CTA per section rather than stacking several.

## Avoid
- Gradient mesh, glow, or "tech atmosphere" backgrounds.
- Stacking multiple CTAs in one section.
- Dense card grids of features — prefer long vertical sections, one idea each.
- Emoji on marketing copy.
