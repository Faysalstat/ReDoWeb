05 · Questions

Answers before you call

Anything not covered here gets answered on the free review.

How long does credit repair usually take?

Most clients see their first deletions 38 to 45 days after the opening round, because bureaus have 30 days to respond plus mailing time. The average completed program here runs 5.4 months.

Can you remove accurate negative information?

No, and no honest company can. We challenge items that are inaccurate, unverifiable or reported past the legal window. Anything accurate and verified stays, and we build the plan around it.

What does it cost?

The opening review is free. Personal credit plans are $99 per month, business credit plans are $189 per month, and tax work is quoted flat once we see the notices. No contract, and you can cancel any month.

Do you handle IRS and state tax debt?

Yes. Florida has no personal income tax, so most local cases are federal, but we regularly handle balances from other states for clients who moved here.

Will filing disputes lower my score?

A dispute is not a scoring factor, so filing one does not reduce your score. Scores move when an item is deleted, corrected, or a balance changes. In rare cases a corrected account reports a newer date and the score dips briefly before recovering.

Do I need to come to an office?

No. Everything runs by phone, email and secure upload. If a document needs a notary, ours travels to you across Orange, Seminole, Osceola and Lake counties.

What do you need from me to start?

A photo ID, one proof of address dated in the last 60 days, and your three bureau report. We walk you through pulling the report on the first call at no cost.

Can you help my business qualify for funding?

Yes. We build the EIN based profile, stage reporting vendor accounts in the right order and prepare financials. Most clients who follow the sequence reach lender ready status in 4 to 6 months.

Are you a law firm?

No. Fix2Live is a credit services organization and tax preparation practice. If a matter needs litigation, we refer you to independent counsel and keep working the parts we can.
